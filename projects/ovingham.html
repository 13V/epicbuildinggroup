Renovation and extension

Ovingham

A heritage street frontage kept, and a two storey extension with a pool built behind it.

Type
Renovation and extension
Built
Jun 2013 to Jun 2014
Our part
Involved in design; full construction

What we did

It began as a simple job, turning a lounge into an ensuite bathroom, and became a large rear extension with a pool. The existing heritage street frontage was meticulously maintained.

Using the sloping block, a two storey contemporary extension was built, with stonework to tie it to the heritage building. The client wanted solid concrete construction to commercial standards and an Olympic size pool.
