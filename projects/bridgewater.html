Extension

Bridgewater

A two bedroom addition to a 1980s cottage, designed and built from the client's rough drawing.

Type
Extension
Built

What we did

The original stone and bricks were no longer available, so we matched the style with retaining wall blocks, cutting each one to size. The roof and the Stegbar windows are matched in as well.

Large inset concrete blocks were used for the retaining wall because of the rocky ground, which saved the client a substantial amount compared with other options.
