Warehouse renovation

Marleston

An existing warehouse refurbished as a gym.

Type
Warehouse renovation
Built
2016
Our part
Refurbishment of an existing warehouse

What we did

Some internal walls were demolished to make a large enough space, and new walls were built. New men's, women's and unisex toilets were installed, with men's and accessible showers.

The main floor is new timber, with impact resistant rubber tile in the aerobic and weight training area. There is a new reception and meeting room, and the existing office was updated.
