Commercial dental surgery

Glen Osmond Road, Fullarton

An existing dental surgery refurbished in three weeks, while it was closed over the Easter break.

Type
Commercial dental surgery
Built
2014
Our part
Assisted with design, internal gutting, reconstruction and fit out

What we did

The building was gutted to its walls and main framework, and all new services were installed, including electrical and plumbing.

Timber windows were replaced with aluminium double glazing to reduce sound. The interior fit out included joinery and bespoke dental furniture.
