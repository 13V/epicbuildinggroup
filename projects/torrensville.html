Renovation and extension

Torrensville

A late 1920s Tudor bungalow, fully renovated and extended, with its heritage features kept.

Type
Renovation and extension
Built
To confirm: year

What we did

Heritage tiles were saved and reused, and new tiles found to match. The new windows match the existing ones and heritage red bricks match the old. The rear roof line was altered to match the front and keep the high ceilings throughout.

The rear extension adds a formal lounge, dining, a kitchen with walk in pantry and a living room, with a new bathroom, powder room, laundry and an alfresco under the main roof. A substantial cellar is reached by stairs down from the main passage.
