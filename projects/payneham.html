Payneham

A contemporary single storey home on a narrow block.

Type
New build
Built
To confirm: year

What we did

Brick veneer with timber framing. Street appeal comes from an off white panel garage door, white columns either side of the front door, and a mix of render and brick.

Four bedrooms, the master with ensuite, a home theatre, and an open plan living, dining and kitchen area with two pairs of stacker doors to an alfresco under the main roof. The kitchen has a butler's pantry and an island bench.

Ceiling level windows in two bedrooms keep privacy from the neighbours. Black sanitary ware, guttering, downpipes and window frames carry the contemporary theme, and the pitched roof was designed to take solar panels later. Fixtures and fittings are from the Epic Silver range.
