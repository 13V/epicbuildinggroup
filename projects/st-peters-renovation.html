Renovation and extension

St Peters

Major upgrades to a bluestone and red brick home, with a new pool, an entertainment room and an undercover barbecue area.

Type
Renovation and extension
Built
To confirm: year

What we did

The aim was to improve the flow of the existing home and give the extended family a large place to entertain, with every change blending into the character of the house.

Built with the Epic Gold range of fixtures and fittings. The detail includes matching Federation skirtings, tessellated verandah tiles, and internal double doors with leadlight glass in the passage. A rear lane gave access for the work and for all services.
