Group development

Ashford

Three three-bedroom homes, arranged on the block for privacy, low maintenance and ease of living.

Type
Group development
Built
To confirm: year
Size
3 homes of about 220 sqm
With

What we did

Each home has ceilings at 2700mm and a roof pitched at 25 degrees, which sits in with the roof heights of the area. Each has a master bedroom with walk in robe and ensuite, a separate main bathroom and a laundry.

A porch over the wide front entrance leads straight into the living, dining and kitchen area, which opens through stacker doors to a rear alfresco. The single garage and alfresco are under the main roof, with an internal door from the laundry to the garage.

External walls are brick veneer, part rendered and part face brick, under Colorbond roofing. The homes were built to the Epic Silver standard.
