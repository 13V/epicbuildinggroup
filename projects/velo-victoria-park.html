Commercial fit out

Velo, Victoria Park

A cafe and cycling gym fitted out in the basement of the heritage grandstand at Victoria Park.

Type
Commercial fit out
Built
2015

What we did

Built for former professional cyclist Stuart O'Grady, former Australian rules footballer Nathan Eagleton and their partners. The brief was to keep the basement itself as a design feature.

We held regular on site meetings to discuss materials and concepts and to explore alternatives. Epic was also engaged by Adelaide City Council, the owner of the building, for acoustic and ceiling works and mechanical services.
