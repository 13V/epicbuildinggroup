# Adelaide competitor websites: luxury custom, renovation/extension and heritage builders

Researched 11 Nov 2026 with Playwright (Chromium, 1440x900 desktop and 390x844 phone). Claims come from screenshots and page DOM. Anything uncertain is marked. Screenshots for the four featured sites are in this folder (turnbull-project.jpg is the projects index, not a single project page).

Not evaluated: epicbuildinggroup.com.au (the client). Candidates that would not load through the sandbox proxy: heritagebuildinggroup.com.au, auracustomprojects.com.au (502). Prostruct was opened but dropped (project management firm, not a residential builder).

## Best 1: Craig Linke, https://craiglinke.com.au (Kent Town / Adelaide)

- **First screen.** No H1 button; the headline is an italic serif statement: "Craig Linke is a boutique, Adelaide based building and interior design company. We specialise in architectural builds and custom renovation projects." Small caption "ENDURING ARTISANAL HOMES" and an "ABOUT US" arrow link. Warm taupe ground, a tall still photo of a kitchen island on the right, a second detail photo bottom left. Header is only logo, a "MENU" word and a small mark. No video (no video elements). Phone: same statement and caption, then the kitchen photo, hamburger menu. No buttons, no phone number on the first screen (desktop or phone).
- **Projects.** Index (/projects) has filters ALL / INTERIOR DESIGN / CUSTOM BUILDS / OUTDOOR LIVING, an "overview" and "index" view, around 12 named projects (The Nest, Hill House, Beach House, Le Jardin, Belltunga, OMH, No. 33, Villa 1890, The Grounds, LWA, Home Base, Victoria Hall) with "Loading more". Project page (Le Jardin, Medindie): a three-column layout. Left column holds facts: CLIENTS (named: Drs Michael and Casie France), LOCATION (Medindie), SERVICES (Custom renovation, Interior design), AWARDS (HIA SA Kitchen Design of the Year 2025; HIA SA Renovation/Addition $500K-$750K winner 2025), PHOTOGRAPHY credit, a "Play video" thumbnail. Centre is a large photo; right is about 3 paragraphs of story. 32 images on the page, a full client testimonial attributed "Dr Casie France, Medindie". No duration, cost, architect, before and after, or plans seen (uncertain: some may sit lower on the page than I read).
- **Trust.** Licence BLD 261831 in the footer and on the contact page. Awards listed per project (HIA). About page: "multi-award winning", states "heritage and contemporary homes", "No display homes. Nothing cookie cutter", names leadership (Craig Linke, Lauren Zwaans CEO, Jake Devlin GM), "Our People" section. Testimonials sit inside projects, one per project seen, attributed with name and suburb. Memberships not seen on the pages read.
- **Enquiry path.** Contact page leads with giant italic "Call / Email / Instagram" links and a form. Fields: Full name, Email, Phone, Address, Services (chips: Interior design, Custom new home, Custom renovation/addition, Outdoor living, Boutique commercial), Total project budget, How did you hear about us, Your message, Submit enquiry. The intro copy ("early planning stages... inspiration pictures, building drawings or Council approvals") is reassuring about the stage a client may be at. Phone 08 7225 4472 is on the contact page, projects index and footer, but not visible on the phone first screen. Budget is a free-text field, not a range selector. No multi-step form.
- **Motion.** Built on Next.js (script paths under /_next/). No GSAP, Lenis, Swiper, Splide or jQuery detected. Layout looks like columns that scroll at different rates (the scroll screenshot shows the featured list sliding in the right column beside a fixed project highlight card); uncertain whether this is CSS sticky or JS. It serves the layout (a showcase) rather than distracting. Project video is on demand ("Play video"), not autoplay.
- **Weaknesses.** Nothing on the first screen tells a visitor what to do next; no phone number or enquiry button until the menu or contact page. Light grey small caps text on taupe is low contrast. The form's "Total project budget" and "Address" asks are heavy for a first contact. The form is minimal and unlabelled on the field itself (placeholder-like labels). Interior design leads the brand over building, which could blur positioning. The home screen has a large empty cell beside the lower photo.

## Patterns the best share

- Photography does the selling: large, well-lit architectural photos by a named photographer, restrained palette, plenty of white or paper space. Names of photographers appear on Craig Linke project pages.
- Heritage and character work is treated as a design story: Urban Habitats' The Pearl text ("revealing the latent potential of an existing heritage home") and Craig Linke's "enhances the bones of the existing architecture" show old-meets-new copy that the weaker sites replace with generic lines.
- Awards sit on the project they were won for (Craig Linke) or up front on the home screen (Urban Habitats), with the exact award and year.
- Licence number is in every footer (BLD 261831, BLno. 159586, BLD 278697, 268558).
- Client names and suburb on testimonials and on project facts (Craig Linke clients, Artesa "Chris & Alex – Fulham").
- Projects are named, not numbered; Craig Linke and Artesa give a fact block (location, services, award, band of cost on Artesa's Hawthorn page).
- A short, forgiving enquiry form: services as chips or tick boxes, a project-type question, suburb, plus a free-text message; Urban Habitats goes further with design-and-build vs build-only, budget bands and timeframe.
- Motion is restrained: no GSAP or Lenis on any of the four; video is used as a quiet loop (Artesa) or on demand (Craig Linke), never as scroll hijack.

## What none of them do well

- A plain first-screen statement plus a visible call or enquire button: Craig Linke has a statement but no button, Urban Habitats has no statement, Artesa has video only, and none show a phone number on the phone first screen (only Buildluxe does, and it is the weakest design).
- Heritage proof: no before and after, no plans or drawings, no discussion of approvals, council or heritage overlay processes, or typical timelines. Project pages almost never state duration or cost (only Artesa's band).
- Explaining the process in steps with timelines and what the client does at each stage; Urban Habitats' "Why Choose Us?" and Artesa's "experience" section are the closest and are still generic.
- Making the call easy on mobile: no sticky call bar and no click-to-call in the header on any of the four featured sites (I found tel: links only on contact pages and footers).
- Covering the full range Epic offers (kitchens and bathrooms, small developments, commercial fit-outs) with credible proof: the best sites are residential, and the commercial work is mentioned only as "Boutique commercial" on Craig Linke's form.
