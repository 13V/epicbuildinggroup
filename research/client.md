# The client: Epic Building Group, as published on epicbuildinggroup.com.au

Read on 11 Nov 2026. Every page in their sitemap (45 URLs) was fetched and read; all photos were
downloaded. Everything below is what the site states. Quotes are verbatim, including their typos.
Nothing here is inferred. Things the new site needs that are not on their site are in `gaps.md`.

  Peter Tsoukalas General Builders (Builder's Licence GL185386 GL/SGL56932)".
- Title tag: "Epic Building Group - Master Builders Adelaide".
- Address: 220 Richmond Rd, Marleston SA 5033. Postal: PO Box 301, Marleston SA 5033.

## Award and memberships

- Footer, every page: "Master Builders Association Award Winner. In recognition of our high standard
  of quality, we were awarded 'Best Renovation' in the category for renovations up to $600,000 by
  the Master Builders Association of South Australia in 2011."
- Parkside project page: "this solid brick project won Epic Building Group the coveted Master
  Builders Award for Excellence in Renovation and Addition ($400k to $600k category)". The two
  statements describe the award differently; see `gaps.md`.
- They call themselves "Master Builders" throughout. The site does not show a Master Builders SA
  member number or logo, and names no other membership (no HIA).

"We specialise in all aspects of residential and commercial construction". Twelve tiles:
Bathrooms & Kitchens; In-House Designer; Renovations/Extensions; Project Management; New Homes;
Group Developments; Commercial Construction; Commercial Fit Outs; Carpentry Services;
Concrete/Civil Works; School Works; Builders Supervision. Each has one or two sentences
(used verbatim or lightly edited on the services page). The about list adds: "Multi Storey Construction", "Budget Group
Developments", "Domestic extensions and renovations (Full Renovation Specialist)", "Retaining Walls &
Landscaping".

Homepage statement: "Deal with traditional South Australian Master Builders. We are there for
regular on site meetings, incorporate the latest materials and technologies and maintain a hands on
approach to keep your project on schedule and budget. We specialise in Adelaide Home Renovations and
Extensions, Bathroom Renovations, Kitchen Renovations, Residential Developments and Commercial
Building."

Finish levels are mentioned inside project text only: Epic "Silver" (Ashford, Payneham, where it is
spelt 'Sliver'), "Gold" (Highgate, St Peters, Goodwood in progress) and "Platinum Range" (Goodwood
new build). They are never explained.

Evidence, each checked in the page source or a real browser at 390 px wide:

1. **Slow.** The numbers above. jQuery 1.8.3 (2012) plus six plugins load on every page, and all
   six 1920 px slides load up front.
2. **No way to call from a phone.** Zero `tel:` links on any page; the mobile number is plain text
   in the footer. The first phone screen has no button at all (screenshot:
   `research/live/home-phone-first.jpg`).
3. **Small text on phones.** Body copy is 13 px. 12 links on the homepage are smaller than a 44 px
   tap target. The viewport tag sets `maximum-scale=1`, which stops people zooming.
4. **The enquiry path is a third-party iframe.** The contact form is a Wufoo embed under another
   organisation's account name; the contact page's own title tag is "Our Team - Epic Building Group".
5. **Thin, unordered project pages.** A typical page is one paragraph and a thumbnail grid of
   740 px photos, all with the same alt text, finished shots mixed with site shots, no captions.
   Newton's page is one sentence with 35 photos.
6. **Errors a client could notice.** Payneham's title tag reads "Goodwood New Build"; Royston
   Park's reads "Newton Group Development"; Rostrevor's dates read "April 2015 - December 2014";
   four photos 404; duplicate project pages; several pages still say "are being built" for work
   dated 2015 to 2017.
7. **Search basics.** `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` point at `http://0.0.0.0:4000/` (the
   developer's own machine), so the sitemap is useless to Google. No structured data. Old Universal
   Analytics tag (retired by Google in 2023).
8. **The testimonial and the award are buried.** The long Payneham testimonial sits in a slider at
   the bottom of the homepage; the 2011 award is footer small print.
